Your library, your privacy.

Updated October 13, 2026

Page Grove HQ is a browser ebook reader hosted on GitHub Pages. Local reading does not require an account. The app does not add tracking analytics.

Device books

Imported files, extracted text, illustrations and reading records stay in this browser. Original imports are retained separately from library backups. Clearing browser site data can remove these copies. Backups are files you download and manage yourself.

Google sign-in and Firebase sync

Optional Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication in the dedicated Page Grove project. With reading sync enabled, Firebase stores book titles, fingerprints, reading positions, sessions, bookmarks, saved excerpts, notes, vocabulary, profiles, preferences, and optional Drive file/folder references. Owner-only database rules restrict access to the signed-in account. Full book text, original files, illustrations and Drive access tokens are not uploaded to Firebase. Saved excerpts and notes may contain text you intentionally selected.

Google Drive

Drive access is separate from sign-in. Folder discovery requires Google's read-only Drive permission, which technically permits viewing and downloading all Drive files. Page Grove scans the folder you select and, if enabled, its subfolders. The app does not upload, edit, move or delete Drive files.

During the first folder check, supported books download temporarily to extract metadata and reconnect matching reading records. Cloud books then download when needed for reading. Full contents remain in memory unless you choose an offline copy or repair text locally. Small covers and reading metadata can be retained on the device. Access tokens stay in memory and are cleared on disconnect, sign-out or reload; reconnect when access expires.

Disconnecting Drive on a device clears its session access. To revoke Google's permission, remove Page Grove from your Google account connections. Disconnecting or signing out does not erase existing offline copies or synced notes. Use browser site-data controls to remove device data.

Other services

Dictionary lookup sends the selected word to the dictionary provider. OCR runs on-device using resources served with the app. Optional AI and server OCR are disabled on this deployment. GitHub, Google and the dictionary provider operate under their own service privacy policies.

Account data requests

Export library backups and notes from the app. Removing an entry from the shelf does not delete its Drive file; Firebase retains deletion markers to prevent older devices from restoring it. Complete account deletion is not automated in this version. Contact the project maintainer through the repository to arrange private account-data removal; do not post private book contents or credentials in a public issue.
